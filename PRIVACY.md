# Markup privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Markup is a Chrome extension for editing copy and leaving comments on web pages. This policy says what data it handles.

## What Markup stores

When you turn Markup on and make a change, it saves the following in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`:

- The text you edited, before and after your change
- Your comments
- The page address, page title, and a CSS selector for each element you changed
- Your settings: on or off, the panel corner, and the copy format

## What Markup sends

Nothing. Markup has no server, no analytics, and no tracking. It makes no network requests. Data leaves your browser only when you click Copy and paste the text somewhere yourself.

## Why it runs on every page

Markup runs on every page so its panel can open on any page you review. Until you turn it on, it does not read or change page content.

## Deleting your data

Click the × button in the Markup panel to delete every saved change. Removing the extension deletes all of its data.
